# Celune Contributor License Agreement

This agreement is based on the Apache Software Foundation Individual Contributor License Agreement (v2.2) and Corporate Contributor License Agreement (r190612). It lets Celune accept your contribution, license it under the Apache License 2.0 with the rest of the project, and, for contributions to files under `ee/`, include it in commercially licensed code.

**How to sign.** Open a pull request. The CLA Assistant bot comments on it. Reply with the sentence the bot asks for:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

Signing covers every contribution you submit to Celune projects on GitHub. If you contribute on behalf of an employer or another organization, that organization must also sign the Entity agreement below before we can merge. Email the completed Entity agreement to hello@celune.ai with CLA in the subject.
