# Analytics & Support

## Analytics

The Analytics section helps you understand how your workspace is performing. You can find it in the left sidebar under **Analytics**.

Analytics has three dashboards, each accessible via tabs at the top of the page.

### Overview Dashboard

The Overview dashboard gives you a high-level snapshot of your workspace activity:

- **Task velocity** — how many tasks your team is completing over time, shown as a trend chart so you can spot patterns week over week.
- **Completion rates** — the percentage of tasks that reach "done" status, broken down by time period.
- **Agent utilization** — how actively your agents are being used across the workspace, so you can see which agents are carrying the load and which are underutilized.

This is a good starting point to check in on overall productivity without digging into details.

### Agent Analytics

The Agent Analytics dashboard focuses on individual agent performance:

- **Per-agent performance** — see how each agent is performing with metrics like tasks handled, response times, and success rates.
- **Health metrics** — monitor whether your agents are responsive and operating normally. If an agent is experiencing issues, health indicators will flag it here.
- **Cache stats** — view how effectively your agents are using cached data to speed up responses and reduce costs.

Use this dashboard to identify which agents might need configuration adjustments or which are performing especially well.

### Cost Analytics

The Cost Analytics dashboard helps you track spending and resource usage:

- **Token usage** — see how many LLM tokens your workspace is consuming over time, broken down by agent and time period.
- **API costs** — track costs associated with AI provider API calls, including trends so you can anticipate future spending.
- **Subscription summary** — view your current plan, what is included, and how your usage compares to your plan limits.

This dashboard is especially useful if you are using your own API keys (BYOK) and want to monitor spending.

### New Workspace Note

If you have just created your workspace, your analytics dashboards will appear empty at first. Analytics begin populating after approximately **7 days of usage**. Once you and your agents have been active for a week, you will start seeing meaningful data in all three dashboards.

## Support

Celune provides several ways to get help and share feedback, all accessible directly within the app.

### Support Chat

Click the **support widget** (located in the bottom corner of the app) to open a chat panel. You can ask your agent questions about how to use the platform, troubleshoot issues, or get guidance on features. The support chat is AI-powered, so you will get immediate responses without waiting for a human agent.

Your conversation history is saved, so you can pick up where you left off if you need to revisit a topic later.

### Contextual Help

Throughout the app, you will find **help buttons** (usually marked with a "?" icon) next to features and settings. Clicking these opens targeted help content specific to the section you are viewing. This is the fastest way to get an answer about a particular feature without leaving the page you are on.

### Support Tickets

For issues that need human attention — like bugs, account problems, or feature requests that require investigation — you can submit a **support ticket**:

1. Open the support menu from the app.
2. Select **Submit a ticket**.
3. Describe your issue and submit.

Your ticket enters a triage queue and will be reviewed by the Celune team. You can check the status of your tickets from the support section.

### Feedback

Have an idea for improving Celune, or want to let us know what is working well? You can share feedback directly through the app:

1. Open the support menu.
2. Select **Feedback**.
3. Write your thoughts and submit.

Your feedback goes directly to the team and helps shape what gets built next. We read every piece of feedback that comes in.
