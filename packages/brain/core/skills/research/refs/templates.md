# Research Output Template

```markdown
# Market Research: <Topic>

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Depth:** quick | standard | deep | exhaustive
**Status:** Complete
**Sources Scanned:** N web pages, N videos, N forum threads

---

## Executive Summary

3-5 sentences. What's the opportunity? What's the risk? Is this worth building?

## Market Landscape

### Market Size & Trends

- Current market size (if quantifiable)
- Growth trajectory
- Key trends driving the space

### Target Audience Segments

- Primary segment: who, what they need, how they currently solve it
- Secondary segments

### Revenue Models in This Space

- How competitors monetize
- What users are willing to pay for
- Pricing ranges

## Competitor Analysis

### Competitor Overview

| Competitor | Type | Strengths | Weaknesses | Pricing | Users/Scale |
| ---------- | ---- | --------- | ---------- | ------- | ----------- |

### Feature Matrix

| Feature | Comp A | Comp B | Comp C | Our Opportunity |
| ------- | ------ | ------ | ------ | --------------- |

### Competitor Deep Dives

(1 paragraph per major competitor — what they do well, where they fall short)

## User Research

### Pain Points

(From Reddit, forums, app reviews, Twitter/X — with source attribution)

1. Pain point — "quote from user" [source]
2. ...

### Unmet Needs

What users want but can't find in existing solutions.

### What Users Love About Existing Solutions

Don't reinvent what works.

### What Users Hate

Opportunities disguised as complaints.

## Use Cases

1. **Use case name** — As a [user], I want to [action] so that [outcome].
2. ...

## UI/UX & Pattern Inspiration

### Interaction Patterns Worth Adopting

- Pattern name — where it's used, why it works

### Visual Design References

- Product/app — what makes it effective

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- Pattern — why it fails

## Value Proposition

### Core Value

One sentence: what we'd build and why it matters.

### Differentiators

What would make ours different from what exists.

### Why Now?

Market timing, technology readiness, or cultural shift that creates the opening.

## Revenue Potential

### Monetization Options

- Option 1: model, estimated range
- Option 2: ...

### Pricing Benchmarks

What competitors charge, what users expect.

## Risks & Challenges

### Technical Risks

### Market Risks

### Competitive Risks (moats, network effects, switching costs)

## Recommendations

### Go / No-Go Signal

Clear recommendation with reasoning.

### If Go: Recommended V1 Scope

What to build first. Keep it tight.

### Key Decisions for the user

Numbered list of decisions that need human judgment.

## Sources

1. [Title](URL) — brief note on what was learned
```
